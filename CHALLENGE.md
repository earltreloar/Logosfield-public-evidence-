# Logosfield Replication Challenge

We invite independent groups to reproduce the following targets using the frozen prereg plans and the provided runners:

- **Mechanism #3:** mean geodesic distance advantage vs matched nulls (shoreline‑/elevation‑/era‑matched).
- **Mechanism #14:** Fisher combined p and trait ranking.
- **CDDR:** η(z) vs 1 under Pantheon+ + BAO compendia with prior variants.
- **SMBH:** Feasibility surface (ε, λ_Edd, duty) under ΛCDM vs Logosfield t(z), plus negative controls.

**What to submit:** effect sizes, CIs, raw/adjusted p, code + environment, notes.  
**Scoring:** closeness to prereg targets; stability under robustness grid.  
**Recognition:** Top replications credited in README.
