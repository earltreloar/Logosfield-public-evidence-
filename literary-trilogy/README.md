## Volume 2 — *The Logos Abides*

The Purgatorio register. The Traveler follows the derivation program from existence (FP-lower forces β>0 — something rather than nothing) toward specificity (FP-upper forces β=2π, d=4 — *this* cosmos rather than any other).

Volume 2 ends at the threshold — where the specificity is visible but not yet fully in hand (HV open). This is the correct ending for the Purgatorio register. The summit is real; the pilgrim can see it; the final ascent is Volume 3.

**Architecture (V22 formal basis):**
- Existence tier (FP-lower): why there is something — β>0, finite entropy, realized structure
- Specificity tier (FP-upper): why this something — β=2π, d=4, the cosmos we are in
- Threshold: Hauptvermutung open — the bridge to full geometric identification

## The Traveler

A man who works with his hands. Who has carried things. Who found, at the bottom of all of it, not emptiness but something that held. He is not Earl explicitly — the same distinction Dante maintained between pilgrim and poet. He carries Earl's weight without being Earl.

*He just followed it all the way down.*
