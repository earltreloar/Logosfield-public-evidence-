## DC-1 — Formal Statement of Ontological Inclusion

### The Question
Does defining L as "all structures satisfying A1-A3" presuppose what we're trying to derive about the Minkowski sprinkling — or is it a legitimate V4-internal definition?

### Finding

The ontological inclusion argument as stated in PR-1 is not circular — but it is unnecessarily doing work that FP-minimal already does more cleanly. Two versions of the argument exist:

**Version A:** FP realizes the Minkowski sprinkling directly → it's in L → FP-minimal forces identity with V4 → PR-1

**Version B:** FP realizes V4's abstract structure → FP-minimal gives uniqueness → PR-1 shows V4's structure *is* the Minkowski sprinkling → the sprinkling is therefore in L retrospectively

Version A has a potential circularity: how do we know FP realizes the Minkowski sprinkling without already knowing they're the same object? Version B is the stronger and cleaner argument — and is what the V30 derivation actually does.

### Resolution: PR-1 Revised Structure

Ontological inclusion is demoted from premise to corollary. P6 is replaced with direct FP verification:

Direct check of FP against the sprinkling's properties, not an ontological inclusion claim.
- **P7:** Exactly one structure satisfies A1-A3+FP. The Minkowski sprinkling satisfies A1-A3+FP. Therefore they are identical.
- **P8:** F2 follows as a property of the identified object.
