## Why V3 Was Superseded

A critical architectural finding motivated the transition to V4:

V1–V3 presupposes the spacetime and gauge structure it claims to generate. The memory-covariant derivative 𝒟_mem,μ operates on fields defined on a pre-existing Lorentzian manifold, with gauge structure from the Standard Model already in place. This makes V1–V3 a *correction-layer theory* — it modifies known physics rather than generating it from below. It cannot answer the question "why does spacetime have four dimensions?" or "why does the Standard Model have this gauge structure?" because those structures are imported as inputs.

V4 addresses this by starting pregeometrically: bare events, a partial order, and a composable memory relation — no spacetime, no gauge fields, no metric. Everything else is derived or labeled honestly as open.
